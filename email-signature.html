Gmail
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings ⚙ › See all settings.
  3. Under Signature, click Create new, then paste.
  4. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
Outlook
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook, open Settings › Mail › Compose and reply (or Signatures).
  3. Create a new signature and paste into the box.
  4. Choose it as your default for new messages, then save.
